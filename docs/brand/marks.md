# Marks

The sun is the one shape every Soleil mark comes from. The studio draws it rising; Clusters draws three of them in orbit. Back to the [brand book](README.md).

## The studio half-sun

Soleil Pictures' mark is a half-sun rising from a flat base: a crown of curved, flame-like rays of unequal length, the tallest at the centre, over a hollow half-disc set into the base.

- **Solid** is the main form. It is the favicon, the link image and the `logo` in the studio's structured data (`assets/soleilpictures_favicon.png`).
- **Outline** is its twin, drawn as a single line (`assets/soleil_pictures_favicon.png`). On posters the outline sun sits small in a corner, above "SOLEIL PICTURES" in small, widely tracked capitals: the YAHWEH poster carries it in grey top left, Deep Dive in white lower right. That stack, sun above tracked caps, is the studio lockup.
- The mark is drawn in `soleil`. Today's files are a lighter amber; that is recorded in [drift.md](drift.md).
- The studio wordmark is **typed**, not drawn: an `h1` reading SOLEIL PICTURES in Brandon Grotesque 700, tracked 1rem (`index.html` and `style.css` at the repo root, `.logo-text`). On the home page the page itself carries no mark, only the typed name and the sun orb behind it. See [pictures.md](pictures.md).

## The Clusters mark

Three suns on a dashed orbit, together forming a C that opens to the right: a large sun upper left, a small one upper right, a medium one lower right, joined by a dashed elliptical arc. Each sun is the three-tone disc: a `soleil` disc, a `sun-cream` highlight overlapping its upper edge, and a `sun-shade` shadow under the highlight. (`boards/public/clusters-logo-dark.png`; the fitted vector copy is `MARK_COLORS` and `sunMarkSvg` in `boards/src/lib/sunMark.js`.)

- **On dark grounds** the dashes are `sun-cream` (`boards/public/clusters-logo-dark.png`).
- **On light grounds** the dashes are `mark-line-light` (`#272727`, a charcoal sampled from `boards/public/clusters-logo-light.png`). The suns are the same in both.
- The interface swaps between them with `.clusters-mark-dark` and `.clusters-mark-light` (`boards/src/styles.css`); a public page that is always dark keeps the dark one.
- The **wordmark** is the mark beside "CLUSTERS" in Brandon capitals, tracked .18em at display size and .16em in the sidebar (`boards/src/components/SoleilWordmark.jsx`).
- The **lockup** is the mark, then the word, on one line, centred vertically: the mark is about 1.15 times the type size (64px beside 56px type at display, 28px beside 24px in the sidebar), and the gap is 14px at display and 8px in the sidebar (`boards/src/components/SoleilWordmark.jsx`).
- On search pages, link previews carry the feel of the mark, suns on dashed orbits, rather than the mark itself (`boards/src/lib/sunArt.js`).

## Wordmarks

Every wordmark is the name typed in **Brandon Grotesque capitals**, tracked wide. None is drawn or redrawn letter by letter, and none substitutes a sun for a letter. The reason is practical: a typed wordmark stays sharp at every size, comes out right in every language the face covers, and needs no asset to keep in sync.

- Studio: SOLEIL PICTURES, alone.
- Products: the short name, CLUSTERS, beside the product's mark. The family name lives in the page title and the endorsement line, not in the wordmark.
- Where Brandon cannot load, the product falls back to its own face chain (`--font-display` in `boards/src/styles.css`); the email sets "CLUSTERS" in system sans at 13px, weight 700, tracked .2em (`supabase/functions/_shared/email/theme.ts`).

## App icons

The mark **full bleed on night** (`#0a0a0c`), from one source, for every platform. Full bleed means a night square fills the icon and the mark sits inside the platform's safe zone: the central 80% circle for a maskable web icon, 66 of 108dp for an Android adaptive icon (new in this guide). The iOS icon is the model: the Clusters mark on night, edge to edge, cream dashes (`boards/ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-512@2x.png`). The splash is night with the mark small and centred (`boards/assets/splash-dark.png`); the native spinner is `soleil` (`boards/capacitor.config.ts`). The Android, touch-icon and splash files that do not match yet are listed in [drift.md](drift.md).

Icon sources live in `boards/assets/`; the web sizes are generated by `boards/scripts/generate-favicons.mjs`.

## Favicons

A favicon has to hold on both a dark and a light browser tab. For a new product, use one form: the mark on a small night tile with rounded corners (about a fifth of its size), one file that holds on light and dark tabs (new in this guide). Clusters ships a transparent mark with cream dashes (`boards/public/favicon.png`, `boards/public/favicon.ico`), which loses its orbit on a light tab; that is in [drift.md](drift.md).

## Link cards

A shared link shows a 1200x630 card (`boards/scripts/generate-og.mjs`, output in `boards/public/og/`):

- night ground, with a soft gold glow allowed in one corner as atmosphere;
- the headline in Brandon, in `ink`, with a quieter subline;
- the product lockup, mark and wordmark, and the domain, small, at the foot;
- **one** gold accent at most, such as the eyebrow;
- where the card shows content, the studio's own films (`boards/public/og/home-v2.png` is the model).

The mark's own gold does not count as the card's one accent. A product that is not about pictures shows its own interface, with the studio's films as any sample pictures.

Scrapers cache a card by its URL, so a changed card gets a new `?v=` number. The studio has no card of its own yet; see [drift.md](drift.md).

## A new product's mark

A new product's mark (new in this guide):

1. Is drawn from the Soleil sun: the three-tone disc of `soleil`, `sun-shade` and `sun-cream`. `sunMarkSvg` in `boards/src/lib/sunMark.js` is the construction of that disc to start from.
2. Arranges the sun to say what the product does, the way Clusters gathers three suns into an orbit. The dashed orbit is Clusters' alone. A product's sun is a whole disc and never sits on a base line, so it cannot read as the studio half-sun.
3. Uses ink as the only other colour (`sun-cream` on dark, `mark-line-light` on light, `#272727`), for lines such as the orbit.
4. Reads at 16px. If the idea is lost at favicon size, simplify the drawing, not the colours.
5. Has a dark-ground and a light-ground version.
6. Becomes the app icon full bleed on night.
7. Is kept as an SVG source, from which every PNG is made. (Clusters' mark has only PNGs and a fitted copy; see [drift.md](drift.md).)

## Clear space and minimum size

New in this guide, kept modest:

- **Around a mark** alone, leave clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
- **Around a lockup** (mark and wordmark, or sun above tracked caps), leave at least half the mark's height.
- Nothing else sits in that space: no text, no edge of a card, no busy part of a picture.
- **Minimum size:** a mark at least 16px on screen; a wordmark set no smaller than 13px, the size the email header uses. Below that, use the mark alone.

## Don'ts

- Don't recolour a mark, or swap gold for an accent a person picked in Settings. Marks stay `soleil`.
- Don't add a glow, gradient, outline or shadow to the mark file. A soft glow behind it on a brand moment belongs to the page, not to the mark.
- Don't stretch, squash, rotate or re-space the suns.
- Don't set a mark on a busy picture without a scrim.
- Don't use an emoji sun, a stock sun icon or a drawn sun in a letter in place of a mark.
- Don't put the studio's half-sun on a product, or a product's mark on the studio's films.
- Don't set a wordmark in Aileron or in lower case.
