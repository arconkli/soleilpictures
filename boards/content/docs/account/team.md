Free and {{fact:planName}} include up to {{fact:editorLimit}} people who can edit
across everything an account owns, and any number of viewers. Team is for the
groups that need more than that: a team owns its workspaces and pays for them
per seat, and admins look after its people, seats and joining.

Workspaces that belong to a team don't count toward the {{fact:workspaceLimit}}
of your own that Free and {{fact:planName}} allow, and a team has no limit on
how many workspaces it owns.

## What a seat is

Everyone who can edit in the team's workspaces holds a seat: the owner, admins,
members, and anyone from outside the team you let edit. An invite to edit holds
one until it is answered. Viewers are free and unlimited.

Seats are {{fact:teamSeatMonthly}} a month each, or {{fact:teamSeatAnnual}} a
year. They are shared across all the team's workspaces, so one person editing
in five of them is one seat. Each seat adds {{fact:teamStoragePerSeat}} to the
team's drive, which everyone in the team shares, and the cards are unlimited.

A team has between {{fact:teamSeatMin}} and {{fact:teamSeatMax}} seats. Seats
nobody uses are let go at renewal, never below {{fact:teamSeatMin}}.

## Starting a team

Choose the number of seats on the [pricing page](/pricing) and press **Start a
Team trial**; signed in, the same setup is at `/team/new`. Name the team and pick which of your own workspaces move into it; the rest stay
yours. Seats start at the number those workspaces need.

The trial lasts {{fact:teamTrialDays}} days. A card is needed to start it and
nothing is charged until it ends. During the trial the team's drive holds
{{fact:trialStorage}}; the full drive comes with the first payment.

If you already pay for {{fact:planName}}, it keeps billing on its own. Once your
work has moved into the team, cancel it in **Settings → Plan & billing**.

## More than {{fact:teamSeatMax}} seats, single sign-on or invoices

[Talk to us](/pricing/talk-to-us), the link under the pricing page's cards, is a
short form: what your team needs, and a work email to write back to. You don't
need an account to send one.

## People

The team's page, at `/team`, has four tabs: **People**, **Workspaces**,
**Seats & billing** and **Joining**. Members and viewers see the first two.

- **The owner** can do everything, and is the only one who changes the card,
  the plan or closes the team.
- **Admins** add and remove seats (adding shows the price first, removing asks
  first), and manage people, workspaces, joining and what happens when seats
  run out.
- **Members** hold a seat, can make new workspaces for the team and can move
  one of their own into it.
- **Viewers** can open and comment, and need no seat.

Everyone in the team sees everyone's name. Only the owner and admins see email
addresses.

Removing someone, revoking an invite or removing a domain shows **Undo** for a
few seconds before it happens.

Admins hear in the bell when people join the team; joins share one notice
until it's read. A team notice in the bell opens the team's page on the tab
it's about.

### Asking to edit

A viewer in a team workspace sees **Ask to edit** at the top of a cluster, as
on a shared cluster. The request goes to the team's admins, who answer it in
**People**, and the person hears back either way.

## Workspaces

The **Workspaces** tab lists the team's workspaces and who can open each one.
A team workspace is open to the whole team by default.

- **Private**: the owner or an admin can make a workspace private. Anyone who
  opens it only through the team loses it; the owner, admins and people added
  to it keep it, so work can always be handed over.
- **Move one of yours in**: pick one of your own workspaces, and the tab says
  first whether the people who edit in it fit in the team's free seats. When
  they don't, the owner or an admin sees the price before seats are added; a
  member asks an admin. The team then owns the workspace, and everyone in it
  keeps what they had.
- **Move out**: the owner or an admin can hand a workspace to one person, the
  team's owner or someone else with a seat whom the owner picks. Anyone who
  opens it only through the team loses it; people added to it directly keep it.

Making a workspace private and moving one out both ask first.

## Joining

Admins choose how people join, and can use any of these together:

- **Email invites**: paste a list of addresses.
- **A team link**: anyone with it can join.
- **Your company's email domain**: prove you own it with a DNS record, and
  people who sign up with an address there can join. Free email providers can't
  be used. People who already have an account with that domain are asked once,
  the next time they sign in; admins never see who has one.

## When seats run out

Admins pick one:

- **Ask first** (the default): someone new joins as a viewer and the admins are
  asked, and can add a seat for them.
- **Add seats automatically**, with an optional limit on how many can be added
  in a month.

## Your own workspaces

The team never sees, opens or bills your own workspaces. While you hold a seat
in a team that is paid up, they get unlimited cards and
{{fact:teamSeatPerkStorage}} more drive (admins can turn this off for the team,
on **Seats & billing**).
If you leave the team, your own workspaces go back to your own plan and nothing
in them is removed.

## Seats & billing

The **Seats & billing** tab shows the plan and its seats, and when the trial
ends and the first charge comes. Once the owner has ended the plan, it shows
the day the team ends, and the owner can keep the team until then. It says how
many seats were added automatically this month. The owner and admins see the
card the team pays with and its recent receipts; only the owner changes the
card.

If a payment fails, the team keeps editing for {{fact:teamGraceDays}} days while
the card is sorted out. After that its workspaces are view-only: everything
still opens, and paying again turns editing back on.

## Closing a team

The owner can close the team. Its workspaces move to the owner's own account,
and everyone keeps the access they had. If that leaves the owner's account with
more than {{fact:editorLimit}} people who can edit, everyone keeps editing, and
nobody new can be made an editor until it is back at {{fact:editorLimit}} or
fewer.
