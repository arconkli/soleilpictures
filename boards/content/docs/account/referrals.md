Invite from **Share** on any cluster, or from **Settings → Invite and earn**.

## How it works

Invite someone who does not have a Clusters account yet: by email from a
cluster's [Share panel](/docs/collaborate/sharing), with one of your links to a
cluster (a view link they join, or an edit link),
with your workspace's [invite link](/docs/collaborate/sharing#inviting-a-whole-workspace),
or with your referral link. When they sign up they get
**{{fact:referralFriendCards}} extra cards** and **{{fact:referralBonusStorage}}
of storage** straight away. You get **{{fact:referralRewardCards}} bonus cards**
and **{{fact:referralBonusStorage}}** as soon as they **actually get started** —
they have opened the app and placed their first card.

When that happens a note lands in your bell, and if you are not in the app at the
time, one email says so too. You can switch the email off in Settings →
Notifications → Invite rewards, or with the link at the bottom of it.

The activation gate is the important detail. The reward is not for a signup, it
is for someone who used the product — which means the number in your stats
reflects people who are actually there.

## Limits

- **{{fact:referralRewardsPer30Days}} rewards in any 30 days.** Friends past
  that still count as got started; their rewards wait their turn, oldest first,
  and land as the 30-day window frees.
- **Only people new to Clusters count.** Inviting someone who already has an
  account shares the cluster with them, but earns no reward.
- **Nothing is paid to, or for, a banned account.**
- Anything held back is checked again every hour, so it lands within the hour of
  coming due.

## Bonus cards and storage

Bonus cards raise your card limit above the free plan's
**{{fact:demoCardLimit}}**. Bonus storage adds to your drive on any plan:
{{fact:referralBonusStorage}} for joining through an invitation, and
{{fact:referralBonusStorage}} for each friend of yours who gets started. Both
stack across referrals and neither expires.

They go to each person's own account, on top of their own allowance, so a free
account with referrals genuinely has more room rather than a temporary boost.
Work inside someone else's cluster still counts against that cluster's owner, as
it always does. See [Cards](/docs/canvas/cards) and
[Plans](/docs/account/plans).

## Earn by working together

Work in the same cluster at the same time as someone else and you both earn
bonus cards while you do: **a card every {{fact:togetherCardMinutes}} minutes**
for each other person working with you, up to
**{{fact:togetherMaxCards}} every {{fact:togetherCardMinutes}} minutes** in a
bigger group.

- **Both of you, right now.** You count while you are in the cluster and have
  done something there in the last {{fact:togetherActiveMinutes}} minutes: an
  edit, a move of your cursor, a selection, a scroll, a key. Someone who can
  only view the cluster counts too. A tab left open in the background does not.
- **Up to {{fact:togetherMonthCap}} cards a calendar month** from working
  together, on top of anything referrals pay. The count starts again on the
  1st (UTC).
- **People, not tabs.** Two tabs or two devices of your own are one person, and
  working in two clusters at once earns once, not twice. Each person needs a
  confirmed email, and nothing is paid to, or for, a banned account.
- **The same bonus cards.** They raise your card limit above the free plan's
  {{fact:demoCardLimit}} and never expire. On Creator and Team they are kept for
  you. They never change how many people can edit a workspace or how many
  workspaces you can own.

On the free plan a small **Together** chip shows beside the faces at the top
while it is happening, with a ring that fills toward the next card. Tap it to
see who you are working with and how many of this month's cards you have.

## Stats

The tab tracks:

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Friends joined | Signed up through your link or your invitation |
| Got started | Of those, how many actually used it |
| Cards earned | Bonus cards credited to you |
| Storage earned | Bonus storage credited to you |
| Free months | Any subscription time earned |

The gap between the first two is the honest one. It is shown rather than hidden.
Rewards still on their way — past the 30-day limit — are listed under the stats
until they land. Below them, a line says how many cards working together has
earned you this month.

## Sharing the link

Share targets are provided for the usual places. On mobile the system share
sheet is used; on desktop, where browsers have no equivalent, pre-filled links
are offered instead.

## Referrals versus collaboration

Two different things:

- **[Inviting a collaborator](/docs/collaborate)** to a cluster is free and needs no referral link. That is how you work with someone.
- **A referral** is inviting someone to have their own account and workspace.

They overlap in one place: someone who signs up because you invited them to a
cluster by email counts as your referral, and so does someone who joins through
one of your links to a cluster (view or edit), or your workspace's invite link, within
{{fact:referralLinkJoinDays}} days of signing up.
The same rules apply, and each person counts once. The Share panel's
**Details**, under its invite box, says so too.
