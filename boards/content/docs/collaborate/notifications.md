Six categories, six independent switches, in **Settings → Notifications**.

| Category | Fires when |
|---|---|
| **@-mentions** | Someone mentions you in a note, comment, document or message |
| **Comment replies** | Someone replies in a thread you are part of |
| **Workspace invites** | You are added to a workspace |
| **Board shares** | A cluster is shared with you |
| **Invite accepted** | Someone you invited opens your link and joins |
| **Invite rewards** | A friend you invited adds their first card and you earn more cards and storage |
| **Product tips and check-ins** | Occasional messages about the product, including a short note from the team when something new ships |

The last one is the one to switch off if you only want to hear about your own
work. The other six are all consequences of something a person did that
involves you.

## Where they appear

**In the app** — unread indicators on the sidebar, and in the browser tab title
for [messages](/docs/collaborate/messages). A [file shared with
you](/docs/collaborate/sharing), a comment on it and notices about your account
show as a toast when they arrive while you are in the app; for a file, **Open**
opens it in the app. A cluster shared with you shows a toast too, with
**Open**. The share itself also reaches you by email, and everything shared
with you is on the Shared page, from **All shared** in the sidebar.

**The bell** — at the top right of the board, right after the theme switch; on
a phone it is in the top bar, just before **Share**. Its number counts the
notifications you haven't read. Press it and the list opens under it, newest
first, with the unread ones marked; **Mark all read** clears the number. Press
a row to go to what it is about: a row for a file shared with you opens the
file in the app, a row for a comment opens the conversation at that comment,
and a note that you earned an invite reward opens Settings → Invite and earn. **Esc**, a click outside the list or the
bell again closes it; on a phone the list fills the screen and **×** closes it.

**Email only when you are away** — an accepted-invite email and an invite-reward
email are each sent only if you are not currently in the app. If you are, the in-app notification is the whole story and
a second copy in your inbox is just noise.

**Browser notifications** — only if you grant permission. Nothing is requested
until there is an actual reason to ask.

**Email** — for things you would want to know about while not looking at the
app.

## Getting back in from an email

The button in a product email goes to `/resume`, not to the sign-in form. Press
**Open my clusters** and you are signed in and taken to the board the email is
about — no code to fetch from a second email, no password.

The link is good for **one press** and expires after **seven days**. Press it
twice, forward the email, or leave it a fortnight and it politely falls back to
ordinary sign-in, which still gets you to the same place.

It only signs you in when you press the button. Loading the page does nothing —
mail providers and security scanners routinely open links in your mail before
you do, and a link that signed you in on load would be spent by a robot.

## Unsubscribing

Product tips, mentions and replies carry a **one-click unsubscribe** that
works without signing in. Invitation, share and
accepted-invite emails are each sent once, for one thing someone did; switch
those off in Settings under Notifications. Unsubscribing from email does not
turn off in-app notifications; those are separate.

## Comments

[Comments](/docs/collaborate/comments) tell the people they concern, wherever
the comment is: on the canvas, on the words of a document or a note, or on a
shared file.

| What happened | Who is told |
|---|---|
| Someone `@` mentions you in a comment | You |
| Someone replies in a conversation | Everyone who has written in it |
| Someone starts a new comment on a cluster, on its canvas or in one of its documents or notes | The person who made the cluster |
| Someone resolves a conversation on the canvas, in a document or in a note | The person who started it |
| Someone comments on a [file you shared](/docs/collaborate/sharing#comments) | You |

You are never told about your own comments, and one comment tells you once: a
reply that also mentions you reaches you as the mention.

Each one is a row under the bell, with the writer's initial, the cluster and
the comment's first line. Press it and the cluster opens with the conversation
open at that comment. Several comments in one conversation while the row is
still unread share that one row, which shows the newest and how many more
there were, rather than filling the list.

While you are in the app a comment notice is also a toast with **Open**, and a
browser notification if you allowed those. A busy conversation toasts once in
a while, not for every reply, and never while you have that conversation open.

Email is narrower, and only for when you are not in the app. A mention is
emailed to the person named. A reply on the canvas is emailed to the person
whose comment it answers, not to everyone in the conversation. A comment on a
file you shared is emailed to you, as [Sharing](/docs/collaborate/sharing#comments)
describes. The button in a mention or reply email opens the comment. A reply
in a document or a note, a new comment on your cluster and a resolved
conversation stay in the bell.

## Mentions

The mention notification is the important one. When someone `@`s you, the
notification links to the exact card, comment or passage, not just to the board
— see [Links and mentions](/docs/organize/links-and-mentions).

A busy thread cannot flood your inbox: one person's mentions reach you by email
at most {{fact:mentionEmailDailyCap}} times a day, and the same goes for their
replies to your comments. Every one of them still shows up in the app.
