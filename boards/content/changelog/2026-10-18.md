### Sharing

- **Share** is easier to find: it carries a little more weight than the icons
  around it in the board header.
- The panel opens on inviting. Type an address, choose **Can edit** or
  **Can view**, and choose how far it reaches — **This cluster**, or for owners
  and co-owners the **Whole workspace**. The line under it says exactly what
  that grants, by name.
- **Who has access** is one list: workspace members, people on this cluster,
  invitations waiting on a signup, and the link as its last row. Each person
  has one menu, to change their access, move them to the whole workspace,
  remove them (with an undo) or revoke an invitation.
- **Copy link** and **Done** stay at the bottom of the panel while the rest
  scrolls. See [sharing and public links](/docs/collaborate/sharing).

### Invitations

- Invite someone new to Clusters and you both get
  {{fact:referralRewardCards}} extra free cards, plus
  {{fact:referralBonusStorage}} more storage each. Theirs arrives when they
  join; yours as soon as they have opened the app and placed their first card,
  with no three-day wait. See [referrals](/docs/account/referrals).
