# Plans and pricing

> The free Demo plan gives you 50 cards or 5GB of files, whichever you reach first — clusters themselves cost no cards — unlimited free collaborators, and any file at any size. Creator costs $25/mo billed monthly or $20/mo billed annually, and changes exactly two things — the card cap is removed, and your drive grows from 5GB to 1TB. Those limits are charged to the workspace owner, so one plan covers everyone working in that workspace and there are no per-seat charges.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/docs/account/plans · Updated 2026-10-21_

Two plans. The difference between them is deliberately small and deliberately
honest — two enforced limits, listed below, and nothing else. The
[pricing page](/pricing) puts the two side by side, and its plain-Markdown twin
is at [/pricing.md](/pricing.md) for anything that reads text rather than pages.

Pressing **Get Creator** there before you have an account signs you in
first, then opens the Creator offer in the app with the plan you picked —
monthly or annual — rather than leaving you to find it. The choice is kept on
that device for 24 hours. Pressing **Start free** instead, there or on any other
page, drops it.

## Demo — free

- **50 cards** to build with
- A **5GB** drive — any file, at any size
- Clusters are free — they cost none of your cards
- Free collaboration — invite editors to any cluster

The free plan stops at whichever limit you reach first.

## Creator

**$25/mo** billed monthly, or
**$20/mo** billed annually
($240/yr, saving $60/yr).

- **Unlimited cards** — the 50-card ceiling comes off, and every
  card you have already made stays exactly where it is.
- **A 1TB drive** — free holds 5GB; Creator
  holds **1TB**, room for the footage, the camera originals and the
  project archives. (During a [trial](#trying-creator-first) it is
  100GB, and the full drive comes with the first payment.)
- **Covers your whole workspace** — everyone you invite builds at your limits,
  and there are no per-seat charges.

## What is genuinely limited

Two things — cards and storage. A free account stops at whichever it reaches
first:

| | Demo | Creator |
|---|---|---|
| **Total [cards](/docs/canvas/cards)** | 50 | Unlimited |
| **[Storage](/docs/files#storage)** | 5GB | 1TB |
| **[File types and sizes](/docs/files)** | Any, at any size | Any, at any size |

Everything else is the same on both plans. [What counts toward
storage](/docs/files#what-counts) — and what does not — is spelled out on the
files page.

A [grid](/docs/canvas/grids) counts the boxes you have filled, one card each,
and an empty grid counts as nothing — so laying out a storyboard before you draw
it costs no cards.

## One plan covers the workspace

Those limits are charged to **whoever owns the workspace**, never to the
person doing the work. So a single Creator plan lifts them for
everyone in that workspace: invite whoever you like, and they build at your
limits without a plan of their own. There are no per-seat charges on any plan.

The same rule is why a free workspace stays limited no matter who is working in
it — a collaborator's own plan never applies to someone else's workspace. If you
work across several, the one that matters is the workspace the cluster lives in.

> **Older accounts keep a higher cap.** The free allowance used to be
> 100 cards. Every account created while that was the
> plan keeps it, permanently — no existing cap was ever lowered. Your own figure
> is on the Upgrade pill and in Settings → Plan & billing, and it is the one enforced.
> [Referral credits](/docs/account/referrals) add to whichever cap you have.

> **Note:** There is no separate cap on clusters — each one is a card, and counts
> as one. Collaborators, editing, sharing, [public links](/docs/collaborate/sharing),
> comments, documents, [screenplay mode](/docs/documents/screenplay) and the
> [API](/docs/api) are not capped at all. No file has a size cap on any plan —
> the drive is the only limit.

## Whose allowance

The **cluster owner's**, always.

Cards and files added to someone else's cluster count against them, not you.
This is what makes free editors work — see
[Collaborating](/docs/collaborate).

## Hitting a limit

You are told when you reach either one, and offered the upgrade — or, when a
Creator drive is full, [more storage](#more-storage). Nothing you
have already made is removed, locked, or hidden. A drop that would not fit is
caught before anything uploads, so you can add the part that fits instead.

Unless you cancel, the files that did not go on wait in that browser tab.
Upgrade, or add storage, and they upload by themselves, so you never drop the
same files twice. A bar at the bottom of the screen says how many are waiting,
with **Upload now** to try again after you make room by deleting, and
**Discard** to let them go. A folder waits whole when none of it fit; if you
add part of a folder, or one runs out of room partway through, the rest stays
on your computer.

While files are waiting, checkout opens in a new tab so this one can keep
them. If the browser won't open one, checkout takes this tab, the files are
let go, and you are asked to drop them again when you come back. Closing or
reloading the tab also lets them go, and the browser asks first; the files
are still on your computer either way. This applies in your own clusters: in
someone else's, only their plan can make room.

Deleting a file gives its space back within a few minutes — unless another
cluster still uses it — and emptying the trash gives back everything in it at
once.

You can also raise your own cap without paying: [referrals](/docs/account/referrals)
grant bonus cards for people you invite who actually get started.

## More storage

If Creator's 1TB drive fills, add more in blocks of
**1TB**: $25/month on the monthly plan,
or $240/year on the annual one, up to 10
blocks. Add a block from **Settings → Plan & billing**, or from the message or dialog
that appears when an upload will not fit. It goes on the card your plan already
bills, with no new checkout. The first charge covers only the rest of your
current billing period, and the sheet shows that amount before you confirm.

If your bank asks to confirm the payment, Stripe opens a page to finish it. The
storage is added once that payment goes through; until then nothing changes.

Remove a block from the same place while your plan is active and not set to
cancel. The unused part of the period comes off your next bill. A block can't
be removed while your files would no longer fit without it, so delete some or
empty the trash first.

Extra storage can be added once a Creator trial has made its first
payment, and only while the subscription is in good standing. It ends with the
plan: if Creator is canceled, the extra storage goes with it.

## Trying Creator first

Once there is a real cluster on your account, or as soon as a drop or an import
is more than your free card limit holds, Clusters offers you
**14 days of Creator** before you pay. The
offer appears where you are already working: on the upgrade pill in the corner,
on the prompts that show up as a cluster fills, when the files you drop will not
fit, in **Settings → Plan & billing**, and on the upgrade screen.

A card is required; nothing is charged until the trial ends, and cancelling
before then costs nothing. About three days before the first charge, Clusters
emails you the date and the amount, with a link to **Settings → Plan & billing**
where you can cancel. It is one trial per account, and it is offered in the app
rather than on this page — it is for people who have built something, or who
are bringing in more than the free card limit holds.

## Billing

**Settings → Plan & billing** shows your plan, the storage meter, any
[extra storage](#more-storage) with the buttons to add or remove it, and a link
to the Stripe customer portal for payment details, invoices and cancellation.
Extra storage is changed in Settings, not in the portal.

Annual and monthly are the same product. Annual is cheaper per month.

## Discount codes

If you have a discount code, enter it at checkout — the payment page has an
**Add promotion code** field. The discount comes off your first month.

Codes apply to the **monthly** plan only. The annual plan is already discounted
against paying monthly, so its checkout does not offer a code field.

## Free forever

The Demo plan is not a trial. It does not expire and it does not degrade. If
50 cards and 5GB are enough for what you do,
that is a complete account. The Creator trial above is separate: it is a taste of the
paid plan, and when it ends you are back on exactly this one.

When a trial or a paid plan ends, nothing you made is deleted. Every cluster,
card and file stays where it is, and you can keep opening and editing it. If
you are over the free card limit or the free drive, new cards or uploads will
not fit until you are back under them.
