## Nine frames, evenly weighted

A contact sheet works because nothing on it is emphasised. Three by three is the point where a frame is still big enough to judge and the sheet is still small enough to take in as one image — which is why it is the shape darkroom proof sheets settled on and why it survived into digital. Every cell here is exactly the same size, so what you are comparing is the photographs, not the layout.

- Nine equal frames, three across and three down
- Drop images straight in — no upload dialog, no export step
- Drag to re-order; the grid re-flows around what you move

## It is a live board, not a flat image

Most contact sheet templates hand you a file you fill in once. This one stays editable: swap a frame, drag one image past another to re-order, or zoom a single cell to full screen to check focus without leaving the sheet. Nothing is baked, so the sheet a client sees next week is the sheet you have kept working on rather than an export you have to remember to redo.

## Grow past nine without starting again

A shoot rarely stops at nine. Stamp a linked copy of the sheet beside the first with the "+" handles and you get a second page in the same grid, aligned to the first. Or split a cell if one frame needs two variants. If you outgrow the shape entirely, pick a different template and everything you have already placed carries across to the new one.

## Share the sheet, not a folder

Publish the board and you get one link that opens the sheet in a browser — no download, no account needed to look. Anyone you invite to edit can comment on a specific frame, so "the third one in the middle row" stops being a sentence anybody has to write.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many images does the contact sheet hold?

Nine by default, in a three by three grid. You can split a cell for more, or stamp a second linked sheet beside the first to continue onto another page.

### Can I re-order the frames?

Yes. Drag one frame onto another to swap them, and the grid re-flows around the move. Nothing is fixed in place once it is dropped.

### Do I need to export the sheet to share it?

No. Publishing the board gives you a link that opens the sheet in any browser, and it stays live — later edits show up without you re-sharing anything.

### Can I use it for something other than photos?

Yes. A cell takes an image, text, a link, a video or a nested board, so the same nine-up shape works for product shots, frame grabs, colour options or candidate logos.
