# Look book template

> A four-panel look book: the cover look top-left, two more beside and below it, and a detail shot to close. Even weighting, because a look book is a sequence of equals rather than one hero. Fill each panel with a photo, a fabric or a still.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/templates/look-book-template · Updated 2026-08-27_

## The layout

2 × 2 — 4 boxes.

| # | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | COVER LOOK |
| 2 | LOOK 02 |
| 3 | LOOK 03 |
| 4 | DETAIL |

Each label shows only while its box is empty, and is never written into the box.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add more looks?

Stamp a linked copy with the "+" on any edge. Each copy carries the same four labels, so a twelve-look book stays consistent without retyping anything.
