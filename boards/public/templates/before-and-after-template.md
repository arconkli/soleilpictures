# Before and after template

> Two panels of exactly equal size, one labelled BEFORE and one AFTER. Equal size is the whole point — the moment one is bigger you are arguing rather than showing. Drop an image in each and the labels disappear on their own.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/templates/before-and-after-template · Updated 2026-08-27_

## The layout

Side by side — 2 boxes.

| # | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | BEFORE |
| 2 | AFTER |

Each label shows only while its box is empty, and is never written into the box.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I stack several before-and-afters?

Yes. Stamp a linked copy below the first with the "+" handle and you get a second labelled pair, aligned to the one above it.
