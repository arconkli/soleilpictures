# Product lineup template

> The four shots a product listing needs, side by side in the order a buyer looks at them: the front, the side, the detail that justifies the price, and one photo of the thing being used. Labelled until you fill each one.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/templates/product-lineup-template · For lining up product shots · Updated 2026-08-27_

4 across — 4 boxes.

| Box | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | FRONT |
| 2 | SIDE |
| 3 | DETAIL |
| 4 | IN USE |

Each label shows only while its box is empty, and is never written into the box.
