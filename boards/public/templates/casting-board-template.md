# Casting board template

> Nine headshots laid out three across, with a row for each tier — leads on top, supporting in the middle, background at the bottom. Everyone reading for the same part sits on one line, which is the comparison a director is actually making.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/templates/casting-board-template · For casting a role · Updated 2026-08-27_
