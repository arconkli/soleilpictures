# Call sheet template

> The page the whole unit reads at six in the morning, in its real order — production and day and general call across the top, then where you are parking, what the weather is doing and where the nearest hospital is, then the schedule, then who is called and when. The hospital box sits up top because it belongs on every sheet, whatever is being shot.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/templates/call-sheet-template · For running a shoot day · Updated 2026-08-28_

Call sheet · header, schedule, cast and crew — 7 boxes.

| Box | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | PRODUCTION · DAY · CALL |
| 2 | LOCATION + PARKING |
| 3 | WEATHER · SUNSET |
| 4 | NEAREST HOSPITAL |
| 5 | SCHEDULE — SCENE, CAST, D/N, PAGES |
| 6 | CAST + CALL TIMES |
| 7 | CREW BY DEPARTMENT |

Each label shows only while its box is empty, and is never written into the box. Boxes not listed are deliberately unlabelled.
