# Storyboard template

> This storyboard template is a three-panel grid: one wide establishing frame across the top, two beats beneath it. Each box carries a label until you fill it, so a stranger opening the file knows what goes where. Add it to Clusters in one click, then drop in stills, sketches or captions.

## Why one wide frame over two

A sequence rarely reads as equal boxes. You establish, then you cut — so the template gives the establishing shot the full width and splits the beat underneath it. It is the shape most storyboards actually take, and it means the first thing a reader's eye lands on is the frame that sets the scene. Drag the divider if your scene wants a different balance; the panels re-flow and nothing you have already dropped in moves.

## The labels disappear on their own

ESTABLISHING, ACTION and REACTION sit in the empty boxes as grey guidance — not as content. They are not text you have to select and delete: the moment a panel holds a still, a sketch or a caption, its label is gone, and if you clear the panel later the label comes back. They never appear in an export, never count toward anything, and never end up in a file you hand to a client.

## One panel is not a storyboard — stamp the row

A scene needs more than three frames. Select the grid and the directional "+" handles appear on each edge: click one and you get an identical, linked panel beside it, carrying the same labels and any numbering slate you have set up. Change a divider on one and the whole row re-cuts together, so a six-panel sequence stays a grid rather than six drifting rectangles.

## Keep the shot list beside the frames

A storyboard without the logistics is half the picture. Put a doc or a schedule card on the canvas next to the panels — lens, movement, location, shoot day — so the visual and the plan sit in one view. Your director, DP and AD can edit the same board at once, and comments land on the specific frame rather than in a paragraph of email.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I use this storyboard template?

Click the button on this page. The template lands in your Clusters templates, and picking it from the grid tool stamps the three labelled panels onto any board.

### Do the panel labels print or export?

No. A label is guidance, not content — it renders only while a panel is empty, and it is never written into the panel itself. It does not appear in exports and does not count toward your card total.

### Can I add more panels?

Yes. Select the grid and click a "+" on any edge to stamp a linked panel beside it, or use the matrix generator to lay out a whole page at once. Stamped panels carry the same labels.

### What if I want a different panel layout?

Drag any divider to re-balance the frames, or split a panel in two. You can also pick a different template later — the images and captions you have already added carry across to the new shape.
