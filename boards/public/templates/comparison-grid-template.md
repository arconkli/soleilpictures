# Comparison grid template

> Two columns, labelled OPTION A and OPTION B, for the decisions a spreadsheet handles badly — the ones where you need to see the two things rather than read about them. Each column takes images, text, links or a nested board.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/templates/comparison-grid-template · Updated 2026-08-27_

## The layout

Side by side — 2 boxes.

| # | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | OPTION A |
| 2 | OPTION B |

Each label shows only while its box is empty, and is never written into the box.

## Why not a spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is the right tool when the criteria are numbers. It is the wrong
one the moment the answer depends on what the options look like — which chair,
which typeface, which location. This puts the two things themselves side by
side, and lets the notes sit under them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What if I am comparing three or four things?

Split a column, or start from a four-across layout instead. Content you have already placed moves over when you change the shape.
