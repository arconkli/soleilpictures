# An Eagle Alternative for Teams That Build Boards Together

> Soleil Clusters is an Eagle alternative for teams: a browser canvas where a crew builds reference boards together, live, and shares them with one link. Eagle is a one-time-purchase desktop library built around one person’s collection, strongest offline and at scale. Keep Eagle as the archive; use Clusters when a team needs the same board.

_Source: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/vs/eagle · Updated 2026-10-02_

Eagle is the best private library for one person’s references. Clusters is the shared room a team builds in — live, in the browser, on any machine.

## Eagle is a library. Clusters is a room.

Eagle is a digital asset manager: a fast library on your own disk of everything you have ever saved, organised by tags, smart folders and colour, and searchable by an image or a description with its AI Search plugin, offline. For one person’s collection it is excellent, and a one-time price makes it easy to own. Clusters starts where a collection becomes a project other people work on: a canvas in the browser where a team arranges the references for this production, writes beside them and decides — together, at the same time.

- Eagle: a private, local library — organised, searchable, offline
- Clusters: a shared canvas — arranged, discussed, decided
- They do different jobs, and plenty of artists keep both

## What a team gets that a library cannot give it

Eagle has no shared, live workspace. Teams share an Eagle library by keeping it on a NAS or in a synced folder, and Eagle’s own support warns about two people writing to it at once. In Clusters the board is the shared thing: live cursors and presence, comments pinned to the card they are about, a vote card beside each option, and a nested cluster per scene or set. A director opens it from a link in any browser, with no install and no account needed to view, and everyone you invite edits free.

- Live cursors, and comments pinned to the card in question
- One link opens a read-only view; invited editors are free
- Runs in any browser; on a phone it adds to the home screen

## Where Eagle is still the better choice

If the job is keeping tens of thousands of references findable — offline, on your own disk, with AI search by image or description and AI Action sorting them by rules you write — Eagle does that and Clusters does not. It also costs US$34.95 once for two devices, against $25 a month for Clusters’ Creator plan. A solo artist building a lifetime library should buy Eagle. Clusters earns its place when a team has to work from the same board.

## How to bring an Eagle project into Clusters

1. **Pick the project, not the archive** — Leave the library in Eagle. Gather the references one project needs — a tag or a smart folder in Eagle is a quick way to collect them.
2. **Bring the originals over** — Eagle keeps every original as a real file on your disk. Select the ones the project needs and drag them onto a new cluster; they upload in parallel and lay themselves out.
3. **Rebuild the structure as nested boards** — A cluster per scene, character or location, nested inside the project, with notes and docs beside the images.
4. **Invite the team** — Everyone you invite edits free, and one link shows the board to anyone, with no account needed to view it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Soleil Clusters a good Eagle alternative?

For a team, yes: a shared canvas in the browser where a crew builds reference boards together, live, and shares them with one link. For one person’s offline library of every reference they have ever saved, Eagle is the better tool, and plenty of artists keep both.

### Eagle vs Soleil Clusters — what is the difference?

Eagle is a desktop library on your own disk: tags, smart folders, colour search and AI search by image or description, offline, for US$34.95 once. Clusters is a shared canvas in the browser: live editing, comments, votes, nested boards and docs, free to start and $25/mo for Creator.

### Can I use Eagle and Clusters together?

Yes, and it is the natural split. Keep the archive in Eagle. When a project starts, bring the references it needs onto a cluster — Eagle keeps every original as a real file on your disk — and build the board with your team there.

### Does Clusters work offline like Eagle?

No. Clusters lives in the cloud and needs a connection, and anything on a board can be downloaded again. If offline access to a large personal library matters most, Eagle is built for exactly that.

### Is there a Clusters app for iPhone or iPad?

Clusters runs in the mobile browser and can be added to the home screen as a web app, where it opens without browser chrome. There is no App Store or Play Store listing yet. Eagle has no official mobile app either.

### How much does Eagle cost?

As of October 2026, Eagle’s store lists US$34.95 as a one-time purchase with free lifetime updates, and each licence covers two devices. Clusters is free to start; Creator is $25 a month for unlimited cards, 100GB of storage with no size limits.
