## What the free plan holds

Two limits, and you stop at whichever you reach first: 25 cards, and 5GB of stored files. A card is one thing on the canvas, so 25 cards is 25 images, notes, links and palettes in any mix, counted across every board you own. That is room for one considered mood board, or a few small ones. It is not room for a whole production’s reference library, and this page will not pretend it is.

- 25 cards, counted across all your boards
- A 5GB drive that takes any file, at any size
- Boards themselves are free, nested ones included
- Up to 10 people can edit with you, and anyone can view

## What you are never asked for

A payment card, a download or a deadline. You sign in with an emailed code, the app runs in the browser, and the free plan has no end date. The one place a card comes up is the 14-day Creator trial, which the app offers once you have built something. Taking it is your choice, it needs a card, and nothing is charged until it ends. Leave it alone and the free plan carries on as before.

## What stays free, whatever you do

Sharing, viewing and working together are not paid features. A view-only link needs no account from the person opening it. People you invite to edit pay nothing, and what they add counts against the workspace owner’s limits, not their own. Comments, documents and public links are the same on both plans. When you reach a limit you are told and offered the upgrade; nothing already on a board is removed, hidden or locked.

## What Creator adds, and what it costs

Creator changes two things: the card limit comes off, and the drive grows from 5GB to 1TB. It is $25/mo billed monthly, or $20/mo billed annually. One plan covers the whole workspace, so the people you invite build at your limits and there are no per-seat charges. Cancel and everything you made stays where it is; you only stop being able to add past the free limits.

Free means something different in each tool, so this is what each one’s own pricing or help page said when it was read on 14 November 2026. Plans change, so check the tool’s page before you choose. On the count of items alone, Milanote’s free plan is larger than ours; the difference on our side is the drive for any file and the free editors.

## Starting free, step by step

1. **Enter your email** — You get a one-time code. There is no password to choose and no payment form to fill in.
2. **Open your first cluster** — A cluster is a board. Making one, or nesting one inside another, uses none of your cards.
3. **Add what the board needs** — Each image, note, link, palette or file you place is one card, up to 25. Your count is on the Upgrade pill and in Settings, under Plan & billing.
4. **Send it** — A view-only link opens the board for anyone, with no account on their side. That costs nothing either.
5. **Decide later, or never** — Nothing counts down. If the board outgrows the plan you are told, and what you made stays as it is whether you upgrade or not.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan holds 25 cards or 5GB of files, whichever comes first, with no payment card and no end date. It is the same app the paid plan uses, not a cut-down viewer. Creator ($25/mo) takes the card limit off and gives you a 1TB drive.

### Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You sign in with an emailed one-time code and start on the free plan. A card is asked for only if you choose the 14-day Creator trial or upgrade.

### How many images fit on a free mood board?

Up to 25, if images are all you add. Every image, note, link and palette is one card, and the 25 are counted across all the boards you own. The boards themselves use none of them.

### Does the free plan run out?

No. There is no trial period on the free plan and no date on which it ends. Your boards stay open and editable.

### What happens when I reach the limit?

You are told, and offered the upgrade. Nothing you made is removed or locked. A drop that would not fit is caught before anything uploads, so you can add the part that does fit, and deleting something gives its room back.

### Do the people I share with have to pay or sign up?

To look, neither: a view-only link opens in any browser with no account. To edit, they sign in, and they pay nothing. Up to 10 people can edit on the free plan.

### How does it compare with the free plans of Milanote and Miro?

As of 14 November 2026, Milanote’s plans page lists its free plan as 100 notes, images or links and 10 file uploads, and Miro’s pricing page lists 3 editable boards with low-resolution exports. Clusters’ free plan is 25 cards with a 5GB drive that takes any file.

### Can I try it without making an account?

In the app, no: a board needs an account to belong to, and the account is free. Without one, an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT can build a draft cluster for you through the no-account address, and you keep it with a link. The AI mood board maker page has the steps.
