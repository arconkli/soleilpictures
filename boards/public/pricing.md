# Start free. Pay when you outgrow it.

URL: https://clusters.soleilpictures.com/pricing

The free plan is a real plan, not a countdown. Creator lifts its limits for $25/mo. Teams with more than 10 people who edit pay $25 a seat.

Soleil Clusters has three plans. Free is $0 forever with no credit card. Creator is $25/mo, or $20/mo billed annually ($240/yr), and one Creator plan covers the whole workspace: up to 10 people who edit build at its limits, with no per-seat charges. Team is for more than 10 people who edit: $25 a seat a month, or $20 a seat a month billed annually, from 3 seats.

## Free — $0 forever

No card, no clock, no expiry.

- **50 cards** to build with
- A **5GB** drive — any file, any size
- Clusters are free — they cost none of your cards
- Free collaboration — up to 10 people who edit

## Creator — $25/mo

Or $20/mo billed annually ($240/yr) — save 20%. Cancel any time — your cards stay yours.

- **Unlimited cards** — The 50-card ceiling comes off. Everything you have already made stays exactly where it is.
- **A 1TB drive** — Free holds 5GB. Creator holds **1TB** — room for the footage, the camera originals and the project archives.
- **Covers your whole workspace** — Everyone you invite builds at your limits: up to 10 people who edit, anyone who views, and no per-seat charges.

## Team — $25 a seat /mo

More than 10 editors. Or $20 a seat /mo, billed yearly. 3 seats or more. Viewers never use a seat.

- **Everything in Creator, for each editor** — Unlimited cards in every team workspace.
- **1TB a seat, one shared drive** — 3 seats hold **3TB**, and every seat you add brings 1TB more. 100GB during the free trial.
- **More than 10 people who edit** — A seat for each, 3 to 100 on your own. Viewers are free and never use one.
- **Admins, and work the team owns** — Admins handle people and seats. Workspaces belong to the team, so the work stays when someone leaves.
- **Their own workspaces, too** — Everyone with a seat gets unlimited cards and 50GB more in their personal workspaces, unless the team turns it off.
- **Join by link or company email** — Invite a list, share a team link, or let in anyone from your verified company domain.

Start a Team trial: 14 days free · card needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is actually limited on the free plan?

Two things — how many cards you can have and how much you can store: 50 cards or 5GB of files, whichever comes first. Any file of any size is welcome on every plan, and clusters are free — they cost none of your cards. People are the same on Free and Creator: up to 10 who edit, and as many as you like who view.

### Do the people I invite need to pay?

No. On Free and Creator, up to 10 people can edit at no cost to them, and anyone can view. What Creator does is carry YOUR limits to them: everything they add counts against the workspace owner, so one plan raises the ceiling for everyone working in that workspace. There are no per-seat charges. More than 10 people who edit is what Team is for, priced per seat.

### What happens when I reach 50 cards or fill 5GB?

You are told, and asked to upgrade. Nothing you have already made is removed, hidden or locked — you simply cannot add more until you free some room or upgrade. Deleting a file gives its space back within a few minutes, and emptying the trash gives back everything in it at once.

### Can I get more storage?

Yes. On Creator, add 1TB at a time, up to 10 more: $25/month on the monthly plan or $240/year on the annual one, from Settings → Plan & billing, on the card you already pay with. The first charge covers only the rest of your billing period, and while your plan is active you can remove a block again, as long as your files still fit.

### Can I cancel?

Any time, from Settings → Billing, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Afterwards everything over the free allowance stays exactly where it is; you just cannot add more.

### Do I need to install anything?

No. Clusters runs in the browser on desktop, laptop and tablet, and the same board opens on all of them. You can add it to a phone home screen as a web app.
